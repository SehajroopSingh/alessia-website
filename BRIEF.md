## The framework: Action × Focus

**ACTION** (the verb — structural spine, drives WORK filters and the colour system):
- **ANALYSE** — researching how a system actually works · violet `#6633C6` / `#9A5BD4`
- **TRANSLATE** — turning complex requirements into something an audience can act on · green `#5AA136` / `#8BC633`
- **BUILD** — building a new system from scratch, tested against real users · blue `#131FAA` / `#228EE3`

**FOCUS** (the subject — descriptive metadata only, plain uppercase text, NEVER coloured,
never a filter row, never a fourth pillar):
ESG & Sustainability · AI · Socioeconomic Development · Behavioural Science

Colour proportions: ~70% neutral / 20% semantic (primaries) / 10% brighter accent (secondaries).
No page reads monochromatically violet/green/blue.

## Positioning

Science · Technology · Entrepreneurship** ("policy analysis" recedes; socioeconomic
development enters). Hero line: "I work at the intersection of ESG regulation, AI
governance and socioeconomic development…". Signature line for hero/About:
*"I work on ESG and sustainability, AI, socioeconomic development and human behaviour —
by analysing how the systems around them actually work, translating what I find for the
people who have to act on it, and occasionally building new systems from scratch."*

## Design (5A/5B)

- Jenna Kutcher reference = typographic confidence/hierarchy/composition ONLY.
- Typography is the primary identity; extreme scale (section headings ~72–110px desktop);
  editorial asymmetry; reduce card UI in favour of editorial rows; typographic chapter
  breaks (ANALYSE. / TRANSLATE. / BUILD.) used sparingly.
- Hero hierarchy: small uppercase metadata → very large serif "Policy, Technology &
  Sustainability" → positioning paragraph → "Policy analyst by training. Builder by practice."
- The central question is the site's biggest typographic moment (THE QUESTION label).
- MemSurf = a deliberate visual interruption in BUILD blue.
- Maya = art-directed spread (THE VEIL WE WORK THROUGH).
- Homepage teasers: WAIFF / CSRD / stakeholder comms (Dreamocracy off the homepage;
  MemSurf keeps its own section). Impact numbers gain: 52% ABCD attendance (86% paid conversion).
